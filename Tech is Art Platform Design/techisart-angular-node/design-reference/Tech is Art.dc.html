About TechIsArt

A publication where what you publish becomes your portfolio.

TechIsArt is a publication and portfolio platform for creative-technology professionals. You build your reputation by publishing what you know, and every piece you publish becomes part of a living record of your work.

01 · Our Story

Built for the people doing the work

Creative technology moves fast, and most of what gets written about it comes from outside the studio. The people who actually build with these tools, the makeup artist who wrote her own try-on software, the producer who knows what a stem splitter misses, rarely have a place to say so with authority.

We started TechIsArt to give them one. A place where knowledge is published with care, edited with rigour, and credited to the person who earned it. Over time, what you publish here adds up to something more useful than a CV: a public body of work that shows how you think.

02 · Our Philosophy

Tech is the boat. You are the sailor.

Technology is a tool, and the human is the master. A good tool takes you further than you could go alone, but it does not choose the destination. We cover AI and every other emerging creative technology on those terms: what it lets skilled people do, and where judgement, taste and craft still decide the outcome.

05 · Our Standards

Use technology well, and say how you used it

Every piece on TechIsArt carries a tools-used disclosure next to the byline. It is required, it is public, and it is never held against the writer. Readers deserve to know how a piece was made.

None
No AI or generative tools were used in the research, writing or images.
AI-assisted
The author wrote it. Tools helped with research, editing, transcription or translation.
AI-generated with human direction
Substantial parts were generated, then selected, edited and verified by the author.

We also credit sources, link to original research when we summarise it, and label all sponsored content.

06 · Write for Us

Share what you know

We publish working creatives, researchers, educators and students who have something first-hand to say. You don't need to be a professional writer. Our editors will help you make the piece as clear as your expertise.

07 · For Brands

Reach the people building what's next

Brands and organisers can list competitions, grants, residencies and launches in Opportunities, where our readers look for their next project. Partner content is always labelled, and editorial decisions stay with our editors.
